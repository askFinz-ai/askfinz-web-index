# Web safety · the Web Index

How askFinz keeps malware, scams and adult material out of its index without quietly deleting the good web — layered checks and a human override.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/web-safety](https://askfinz.com/web-safety)

## Key points

### How it works

No single check is trusted on its own, because no single check is reliable on its own. Each layer catches what the others miss, and the last one is a person.

### What we go by

Any keyword blocklist can keep out pornography. It will also keep out the sexual-health charity, the addiction-recovery group and the medical reference — and nobody will notice until somebody needed one of them.

### Live · the numbers

Safety claims are easy to make and hard to check, so these come straight from the system rather than from a slide. “Hidden by default” means indexed but kept out of ordinary results — out of the way rather than erased.

### Both kinds of mistake matter

Something in the index that shouldn't be, or something missing that should be there — we want to hear about either. A filter nobody can correct isn't a safety feature, it's just an opinion nobody voted for.

## Frequently asked

### What exactly gets kept out?

Sites whose purpose is to serve the thing: pornography, casinos and bookmakers, illegal drug sales, and malware, phishing or scam operations. Sites that discuss, treat, report on or help people with those subjects are not removed.

### Doesn't filtering just delete things you disagree with?

That's the risk, and it's why the burden of proof sits with removal. Automated checks flag; they don't decide alone. A removal needs two independent judgements to agree, public-interest domains like universities and health services are exempt from automated removal entirely, and a person can reverse any of it.

### What if a legitimate site gets caught?

It happens, and we treat it as a fault rather than an acceptable cost. Industry threat lists are aggressive by design — we've seen them flag a national bank, a university and a medical reference. That's why those lists inform a review rather than triggering a block, and why the human override exists.

### Can I turn the filtering off?

Ordinary results are filtered by default. Material that's been indexed but hidden can still be reached deliberately — it's out of the way, not erased. Genuinely harmful content isn't indexed at all, so there's nothing to turn back on.

### How do I report something you've got wrong?

Either direction — something that shouldn't be there, or something missing that should be — get in touch. Both are worth knowing about, and both get looked at by a person.
