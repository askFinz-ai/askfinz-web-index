01 It joins the queue Your domain goes straight onto the list of sites waiting to be looked at. Nothing to sign, nobody to email.

### Why domains, not pages

It's the obvious thing to ask: why not just submit the URL you care about? Because a page is a fragment of an argument the rest of the site is making.

### Owners get more say than this

Suggesting a domain is the anonymous route. If the site is yours, you can decide how it's read, be read directly with priority and real-time freshness, or ask us not to read it at all.

## Frequently asked

### Do I have to own the domain to submit it?

No. Anyone can suggest a site worth reading. If you do own it, there's a separate route that gets your site read directly and kept fresh with priority — see the partner page.

### Can I submit a single page instead?

Not through this form. Submissions are per-domain because a page read on its own tends to lose the context that makes it useful. Submitting the domain gets that page read anyway, along with everything around it that explains it.

### How long until it's indexed?

Accepted domains normally begin being read within minutes rather than days. How long a whole site takes depends on its size and how fast it's happy to be read.

### What if my domain is turned down?

Some sites aren't a fit — link shorteners, tracking domains, storefront spam and anything with no readable content. The form tells you at the time rather than leaving you waiting.
